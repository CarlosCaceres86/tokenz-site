# Asset provenance
All graphical resources are existing Tokenz app illustrations, reused for the owner-authorized public Tokenz website. No new raster artwork was generated. Tokenz name, logo and illustrations are reserved to their owner; public source visibility does not grant a license to reuse the brand/artwork. No open-source license is added to these resources.

Source checkout: Tokenz canonical app repository, September 2026. mascot.png, star.png and wordmark.png: assets/branding/tokenz-{mascot,star,wordmark}-transparent.png. Avatar and catalog PNG: corresponding assets/optimized files (avatar_girl_ponytail, norm_chores, norm_reading, reward_pizza). Copies are byte-identical; originals are not edited.

Nunito Regular/Bold/ExtraBold are copied from KMP design-system composeResources/font. Copyright and OFL1.1 permission notices are preserved in fonts/OFL.txt. Fonts are served locally, with no external font requests.

Additional byte-identical app resources: teeth.png from norm_teeth.png; avatar-leo.png from avatar_boy_teal.png; family.png from dialog_children_plus.png, all in assets/optimized.
