# Asset provenance
All graphical resources are existing Tokenz app illustrations, reused for the owner-authorized public Tokenz website. No new raster artwork was generated. Tokenz name, logo and illustrations are reserved to their owner; public source visibility does not grant a license to reuse the brand/artwork. No open-source license is added to these resources.

Source checkout: Tokenz canonical app repository, October 2026. mascot.png, mascot-guide.png and mascot-celebrate.png are byte-identical copies of assets/optimized/mascot_{welcome,guide,celebrate}.png, the approved 2D app family. star.png is assets/optimized/points_token.png; wordmark.png is assets/branding/tokenz-wordmark-transparent.png. Avatar and catalog PNG: corresponding assets/optimized files (avatar_girl_ponytail, norm_chores, norm_reading, reward_pizza). Copies are byte-identical; originals are not edited.

Nunito Regular/SemiBold/Bold/ExtraBold are copied from KMP design-system composeResources/font. Copyright and OFL1.1 permission notices are preserved in fonts/OFL.txt. Fonts are served locally, with no external font requests.

Additional byte-identical app resources: teeth.png from norm_teeth.png; avatar-leo.png from avatar_boy_teal.png; family.png from dialog_children_plus.png, all in assets/optimized.

Additional byte-identical app resources from assets/optimized: board-background.png (background_main_overlay), menu.png (ui_hamburger), norms.png (section_norms_checklist), rewards.png (section_rewards_gift), lock.png (ui_lock). All use the existing app pipeline outputs.

Native product screenshots and their capture record are in screenshots/PROVENANCE.md. These are Simulator captures, not redrawn mockups.
